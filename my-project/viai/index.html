02The gap

Stations produce data. A clinic runs on a journey.

Every athlete who passes through V‑Clinic has a before, a during and an after. The stations cover the middle. Three questions decide whether the clinic scales.

Before the visit

Who collects the history?

Injuries, training load, sleep, goals, current protocol. The clinician needs it before the athlete steps on the treadmill.

Today: a form at the front desk, or nothing.

During the visit

Who writes the report?

The physio talks to the athlete, reads five station outputs and has to produce a structured conclusion.

Today: the physio, after hours.

After the visit

Who watches the protocol?

Is the recovery plan followed? What do the glasses and the ring say a week later? Who reacts if something drifts?

Today: nobody, until the next visit.

Our reading

A semi‑autonomous clinic needs an autonomous layer around the stations. That layer is what we have already built and run.
