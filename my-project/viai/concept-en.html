02The continuous loop

A continuous intelligence layer around V‑Clinic

One shared context links preparation, screening, the clinician’s decision, plan adherence and reassessment.

03Living profile

One profile. Every interaction adds context.

Not another dashboard, but a persistent model of the person, their plan and their response to interventions.

Baselinegoals · history · limitations · past injuries
ScreeningV‑Clinic data · VO₂ · movement · biometrics
Daily signalssleep · HR · activity · training · symptoms
Interventionsprotocols · recommendations · plan adherence
Outcomeschange · response · recovery · new baseline
Questions the system can answer
  1. What has changed since the last screening?
  2. Is the person following the agreed plan?
  3. Which signals need the clinician’s attention?
  4. How did the intervention affect recovery?
  5. With what context should the next visit begin?

06How to start

One workflow. 6–8 weeks. 20–50 users.

We do not replace the VIAI platform. We assemble one closed scenario around V‑Clinic and measure whether it creates value.

Success criteriaclinician time · protocol adherence · engagement after screening · return visit · volume of usable longitudinal data

If the loop works, we scale it to other scenarios and locations. If it does not, we stop without a large platform bet.
